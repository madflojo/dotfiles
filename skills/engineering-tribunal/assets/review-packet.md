# Review packet

- Review ID, lifecycle, round, mode, question, facilitator, reviewer due signal, replacement bounds.
- Target and reviewed revision; acceptance criteria and intended next checkpoint.
- Applicable rules and actual decision/exception authorities; unknown authority gaps.
- Requirements, hard constraints with sources, affected scope/owners, evidence, assumptions, unknowns.
- Decision mode: meaningful options, including current state where useful; benefits, costs, rationale, consequences.
- Artifact mode: artifact and criteria to assess.
