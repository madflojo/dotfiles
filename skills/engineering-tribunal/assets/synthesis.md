# Tribunal synthesis

- Review ID, lifecycle, round, target revision, packet digest, and scope.
- Required/received valid assessments, identities, lenses, coverage gaps, and RACI selection rationale.
- Independent review status: complete, incomplete, or invalidated.
- Target recommendation and decision disposition: separately state ready/changes required/blocked and pending/approved/rejected/deferred, with actual authority evidence if supplied.

## Findings and dispositions

| Finding | Reviewer | Severity/blocking | Evidence and consequence | Disposition and rationale | Owner and next action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Preserve individual rankings or criterion assessments, agreement, dissent, conditional convergence, and unresolved blockers. Explain the simplest viable approach and any justified complexity.

## Consumer evidence and verification

Record checkpoint status, intended population/task, evaluated revision, observations, missing evidence, and the claims supported or still provisional. List actual checks/results separately from reviewer judgment and planned checks.

## Residual risk and handoff

For each material residual risk: recommend acceptance or rejection, explain why, identify the competent owner, and name the next action/revalidation trigger. State whether a bounded second round or new lifecycle is needed. Never convert this recommendation into approval by implication.
